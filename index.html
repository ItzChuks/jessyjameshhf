Who we are

A helping hand for every home.

Jessy James Helping Hands Foundation brings neighbours together to save with discipline and support one another. Members contribute small amounts on a schedule that fits them, and when their plan ends they collect a stocked kitchen, without the stress of saving alone.

Community building

Saving together keeps families fed, strengthens trust between neighbours and builds habits that last.

Empowerment

A steady payout gives members the room to restock a business, pay school fees or plan ahead with confidence.

Transparent savings

Every member gets a personal wallet, funded by bank transfer with a receipt. Every naira in and out is recorded on your dashboard with receipts.

How it works

Four steps from sign-up to payout.

  1. 01

    Create an account

    Sign up with your name, email, country and phone number, and accept the terms. Your dashboard is ready straight away.

  2. 02

    Fund your wallet

    Open Wallet, transfer to the foundation account and upload your receipt. No BVN needed. A tax charge applies to each deposit.

  3. 03

    Join your plans

    Pick a 4, 8, 12 or 25-week plan. You pay the joining fee and your first payment when you join. Got a plan code? Enter it then.

  4. 04

    Collect your payout

    When the plan is complete and another plan has been running for 2 weeks, request your payout. T-shirt and clearance fees apply.

Fees and rules

Clear fees, no surprises.

Please read these before you join. You will be asked to accept them when you create your account.

Tax charge

Added to every deposit into your wallet. To add you pay ; your wallet gets the full amount.

T-shirt registration

One time only. You can use your dashboard and join plans without it; it is required only when you request a payout.

Joining fee

Taken from your wallet each time you join a plan, separately from your contributions.

Payout clearance fee

Taken from your wallet with every payout request. Your request is sent as soon as it is paid.

  • Your first payment is made the day you join. Weekly plans are then paid every 7 days from that day (a 4-week plan joined on the 1st pays on the 1st, 8th, 15th and 22nd); daily plans every day.
  • Payouts open a set number of days after joining, shown on each plan (for example 30 days for a 4-week plan).
  • Every plan you join gives you a plan code, usable from a set day after joining until it expires (shown on each plan). Use it or share it when joining a new plan. It works once, then shows as used; unused codes show as expired.
  • On each due day your payment must be made before 12:00 noon (it is taken from your wallet from midnight). Still unpaid at 12 noon, it costs double; the penalty does not count towards your savings.
  • A payout can be requested only when the plan is fully paid and its payout date has been reached.
  • To request a payout, you must have another plan that has been actively running for at least 2 weeks, and your bank details saved on your Profile.
