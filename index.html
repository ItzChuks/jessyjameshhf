Who we are

A helping hand for every home.

Jessy James Helping Hands Foundation brings neighbours together to save with discipline and support one another. Members contribute small amounts on a schedule that fits them, and at the end of the season they collect a lump sum or a stocked kitchen, without the stress of saving alone.

Community building

Saving together keeps families fed, strengthens trust between neighbours and builds habits that last.

Empowerment

A steady payout gives members the room to restock a business, pay school fees or plan ahead with confidence.

Transparent savings

Every member gets a personal wallet and account number. Every naira in and out is recorded on your dashboard with receipts.

How it works

Four steps from sign-up to payout.

  1. 01

    Create an account

    Sign up with your name, email, country and phone number, and accept the terms. Your dashboard is ready straight away.

  2. 02

    Fund your wallet

    You get your own fixed account number. Transfer money to it from any bank app and your wallet is credited in real time.

  3. 03

    Join your plans

    Pick one or more plans. A joining fee applies per plan, and contributions are taken from your wallet automatically.

  4. 04

    Collect your payout

    When the plan is complete and another plan is running, request your payout. The one-time t-shirt fee and a clearance fee apply.

Fees and rules

Clear fees, no surprises.

Please read these before you join. You will be asked to accept them when you create your account.

T-shirt registration

One time only. You can use your dashboard and join plans without it; it is required only when you request a payout.

Joining fee

Taken from your wallet each time you join a plan, separately from your contributions.

Payout clearance fee

Taken from your wallet with every payout request. Your request is sent as soon as it is paid.

  • Daily plans are due before 12:00 noon, weekly plans by Friday 12:00 noon, and monthly plans by the 2nd of the next month (Lagos time).
  • A contribution collected after its deadline costs double. The penalty does not count towards your savings.
  • A payout can be requested only when the plan is fully paid and its saving period has ended.
  • To request a payout, you must have another plan actively running.
